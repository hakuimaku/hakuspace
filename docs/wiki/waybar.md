# Waybar and Cava Underbar

## Changing the Waybar layout

Press `SUPER + SHIFT + W` to cycle through the layouts. You can also use `Haku Menu > Theme > Switch Waybar Theme` to choose one directly.

The default layouts are `top`, `left`, `coredge`, `minimal`, `full`, `neon`, and `island`.

## Cava Underbar

Cava Underbar displays an audio visualizer below Waybar. Toggle it with `SUPER + T`, or use `Haku Menu > Theme > Cava Underbar`.

If the visualizer is empty, check that Cava and the `waybar-cava` plugin are installed. Audio must also be playing through PipeWire/PulseAudio.

## Information on Waybar

Depending on the layout, Waybar can display workspaces, notifications, volume, network, battery, the clock, and recording status. Click a module to open its related tool when supported.
