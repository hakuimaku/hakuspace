# Screenshots, recording, and clipboard

## Screenshots

- `SUPER + P`: drag to select the area to capture.
- `SUPER + SHIFT + P`: capture the full screen.

Images are saved in `~/Pictures/Screenshots` and copied to the clipboard. Open this folder in Thunar to review them.

On Niri, you can also use `Print`, `CTRL + Print`, or `ALT + Print` for the available capture scopes.

## Screen recording

Press `SUPER + F11` or choose `Screen Record` in Haku Menu. When prompted, choose one of these modes:

- `Only Sound`: record system audio.
- `Micro and Sound`: record the microphone and system audio.
- `No Sound`: record video only.

Videos are saved in `~/Videos`. Use the shortcut again to stop recording.

## Clipboard history

- `SUPER + V`: open copied items and choose one to copy again.
- `SUPER + SHIFT + V`: clear the entire history.

Do not copy passwords or sensitive data if you do not want them to appear in the history.
