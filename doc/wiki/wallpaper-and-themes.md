# Wallpapers and themes

## Static wallpapers

1. Copy an image into `~/Pictures/Wallpapers`.
2. Press `SUPER + Y`.
3. Choose the image from the list.

You can also use `Haku Menu > Theme > Change Wallpaper`.

## Automatic wallpaper changes

Go to `Haku Menu > Theme > Auto Random Wallpaper` to toggle this feature. When enabled, a random wallpaper is selected every five minutes by default.

## Video wallpapers

1. Copy a video into `~/Videos/Wallpapers`.
2. Press `SUPER + SHIFT + Y`.
3. Choose the video to play.

To stop the video, use `Haku Menu > Theme > Kill Lively Wallpaper`.

## Accent colors and themes

When you change the wallpaper, HakuSpace can extract a prominent color from the image and apply it to Waybar, Rofi, SwayNC, Kitty, and other interface components. Go to `Haku Menu > Theme > Change Theme` to change the color or font manually.

If the color does not update, choose the wallpaper again or log out and back in.
